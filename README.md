## About

I am a resourcing and workforce planning professional with 8+ years across EY and Capgemini, focused on strategic staffing, demand forecasting, capacity planning, and talent deployment for consulting teams.

My work sits between people, delivery, and data: making demand visible, keeping resource decisions commercially grounded, and helping stakeholders move faster without losing the human context behind staffing choices.

## Signature Impact

| Focus | What I bring |

| Workforce analytics | Weekly Ops MI, utilization trends, forecasted chargeable hours, bench reviews, and leadership-ready performance narratives. |
| Cross-region operations | UK&I and GDS collaboration, including a 3-month London secondment supporting forecasting, knowledge transfer, and delivery alignment. |
